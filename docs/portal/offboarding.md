# Offboarding checklist

No offboarding workflow exists. For an authorized offboarding, record approver and effective time; disable account; revoke sessions and identity access; remove role/team grants; reassign pending reviews; revoke future attachment access; preserve required records under approved retention/legal hold; return company assets through existing company process; and audit the change. Do not delete records or make a former supervisor's new scope expose historical records automatically. Define historical visibility policy and test it before launch.
