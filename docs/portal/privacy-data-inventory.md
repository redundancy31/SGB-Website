# Privacy data inventory (draft)

| Data | Purpose | Proposed access | Current state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity email and provider subject | Authentication and notices | Identity service; limited portal operators | Provider not configured |
| Name, employee ID, role/team, employment dates/status | Employment administration and self-service | Worker own; supervisor operational subset; HR | Not collected |
| Contact details | Work communication | Worker own; HR as approved | Not collected |
| Skills/certifications and evidence | Competency tracking | Worker own; designated reviewer | Not collected |
| Claims, amounts, receipts | Reimbursement review | Worker own; supervisor/finance scoped | Not collected |
| Sick absence dates, MC document, verification | Absence operations and verification | Worker; designated medical/HR reviewer; supervisor operational minimum | Not collected |
| Leave requests/balance ledger | Leave administration | Worker own; assigned reviewer; HR | Not collected |
| Timesheets | Work record review | Worker own; assigned supervisor; HR if approved | Not collected |
| Audit/security events | Accountability and incident response | Scoped authorized roles | Not collected |

Do not collect national identity numbers, bank details, diagnoses, or unrelated sensitive information without a documented necessity and approved controls. Controller/contact details, purposes, legal basis/notice language, processors, overseas transfers, retention, access/correction process, and breach workflow require company/DPO review. This draft is not legal advice or a compliance determination.
