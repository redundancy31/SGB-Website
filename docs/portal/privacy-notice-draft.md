# Employee privacy notice (draft for company/DPO review)

**Not approved and not in force.** The portal is not currently collecting employee data.

SGB Engineering intends to use an invitation-only employee portal for account access, employment self-service, skills/certifications, expense claims, leave, sickness reporting, timesheets, approvals, notifications, audit, and security. The final notice must identify the responsible legal entity and contact, data categories and purposes, applicable basis, recipients/processors, overseas transfers and safeguards, retention, access/correction channels, security contact, and complaint/escalation route.

Do not publish this draft until HR/DPO confirms accuracy and applicable law. Do not submit medical details except through an approved restricted workflow once available. Privacy request handling is not implemented.
